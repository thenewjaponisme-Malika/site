About

"The New Japonisme" is a journal of Japanese culture, seen and studied through foreign eyes for an international community.

From everyday lifestyle and food rituals to philosophy, vintage aesthetics, and modern style, the blog shares authentic stories of how Japan is lived today. It explores the balance between tradition and the present, showing how design, food, and thought continue to shape daily life.

This is not a guidebook or a postcard image.
It is a space to discover Japan as a living culture, where small details carry meaning and everyday choices reflect larger ideas.

"The New Japonisme" invites readers to explore style, food, and the art of living in Japan with curiosity and openness.
